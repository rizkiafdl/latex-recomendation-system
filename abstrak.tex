\begin{center}
    \textbf{\textit{ABSTRACT}}
\end{center}

Manual mapping of internship supervisors at XYZ University is inefficient and causes expertise mismatches. This study develops a web-based decision support system using semantic similarity (BAAI/bge-m3 model), integrated with a company group bonus and a two-phase greedy allocation algorithm. Evaluation involving 168 students achieved an outstanding MRR of 0.711 and a Hit@5 of 0.929. Furthermore, the system successfully balanced supervisor workloads, achieving a near-perfect Gini Coefficient of 0.0138. The prototype significantly enhances the efficiency, consistency, and transparency of academic supervisor assignments.  \par
\textit{\noindent\textbf{Keywords}: Supervisor Recommendation System, Semantic Similarity, Company Group Bonus, Natural Language Processing, Decision Support System, Internship Program.}

\begin{center}
    \textbf{ABSTRAK}
\end{center}

Pemetaan manual Faculty Supervisor magang di XYZ University kurang efisien dan memicu ketidaksesuaian kompetensi. Penelitian ini mengembangkan decision support system berbasis web menggunakan pendekatan semantic similarity (model BAAI/bge-m3) yang diintegrasikan dengan company group bonus dan algoritma alokasi greedy dua fase. Evaluasi terhadap 168 mahasiswa menunjukkan performa retrieval unggul dengan nilai MRR 0,711 dan Hit@5 0,929. Selain itu, sistem berhasil memastikan pemerataan beban bimbingan dosen yang hampir sempurna dengan nilai Gini Coefficient sebesar 0,0138. Sistem ini terbukti meningkatkan efisiensi, konsistensi, dan transparansi proses administrasi akademik.\par

\textit{\noindent\textbf{Keywords}: Sistem Rekomendasi Faculty Supervisor, Semantic Similarity, Company Group Bonus, Natural Language Processing, Decision Support System, Program Enrichment}
